## Q13: Describe your experience porting 1,000+ numbers globally. Do you have a dedicated "Porting Desk"?

**Full Question**: Describe your experience porting 1,000+ numbers globally. Do you have a dedicated "Porting Desk"?

**Importance**: Critical

**Answer**:

Yes. Twilio has extensive experience managing enterprise-scale porting projects involving 1,000+ numbers across multiple countries and regions. For complex, multi-country migrations, Twilio offers a premium white-glove Custom Porting service that provides dedicated account management and proactive support throughout the entire porting process.

With Custom Porting, customers receive a dedicated project manager as their single point of contact who coordinates all aspects of the migration: project scoping, document validation (LOAs, invoices, regulatory forms), carrier coordination across multiple providers, bulk processing workflows, scheduled port dates and times, and post-port testing. This service includes proactive communication at every stage and the ability to schedule regular meeting cadences.

Twilio maintains direct relationships with carriers and regulatory authorities in over 100 countries, enabling streamlined processes and rapid escalation resolution. Custom Porting is a paid service priced based on project complexity (number count, countries, carriers involved, and regulatory requirements).

## Q14: What is your estimated "lead time" (in days) for porting in our countries?

**Full Question**: What is your estimated "lead time" (in days) for porting in our countries?

**Importance**: Critical

**Answer**:

Porting lead times vary significantly based on several factors: number type (local, mobile, toll-free), country-specific regulations, losing carrier requirements, and documentation completeness. Lead times can range from a few days to several weeks depending on these variables.

We recommend providing the specific list of numbers for an accurate timeline assessment. Through our Custom Porting service, your dedicated project manager will evaluate the scope, identify country and carrier-specific requirements, and provide realistic timelines for each phase of the migration.

The project manager will coordinate with losing carriers, plan porting schedules, and work with your team to align migration dates with your business needs. This ensures you have accurate expectations and can plan your cutover accordingly.
