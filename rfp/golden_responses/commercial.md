## Q57: Confirm your "Price per Minute" model. Specify the billing increment. If you do not provide a price ...

**Full Question**: Confirm your "Price per Minute" model. Specify the billing increment. If you do not provide a price per minute, what is your model and how is it better than a price per minute

**Importance**: Critical

**Answer**:

SIP Trunking Pricing Model & Billing Methodology

Twilio Elastic SIP Trunking is delivered through a usage-based “price per minute” commercial model.

Default Model: Twilio’s standard pricing for voice and Conversation Relay is based on a “price per minute” model.
Billing Increment: The default billing increment is 60+60 seconds. This means:
The first increment is 60 seconds (1 minute).
Each additional increment is also 60 seconds.
Any partial minute is rounded up to the next full minute.
Example: A 1 minute and 5 second call is billed as 2 minutes.
Example
A 5-second call is billed as 1 minute.
A 1 minute and 20 second call is billed as 2 minutes.

Charges are typically applied based on:

Inbound and outbound voice usage
Destination country and call termination geography
Applicable carrier routing and regulatory requirements
Associated number services and configuration where relevant
Billing Increment

Twilio Elastic SIP Trunking voice traffic is generally billed on a per-minute basis using 60-second billing increments, unless otherwise specified within country-specific pricing schedules or commercial agreements.

Commercial Model & Customer Benefits

Twilio’s SIP Trunking model is designed to provide transparency, scalability and commercial flexibility aligned with actual voice consumption.

Unlike traditional telephony models relying on fixed circuits, reserved capacity or long-term infrastructure commitments, Twilio’s usage-based pricing enables organisations to scale voice capacity dynamically and pay according to actual utilisation.

This approach provides several advantages:

Commercial Transparency – clear and predictable visibility into usage and cost drivers
Elastic Scalability – capacity scales dynamically without infrastructure constraints
Operational Flexibility – simplified expansion and adaptation across international markets
Reduced Infrastructure Overhead – avoidance of fixed telephony capacity models and associated operational complexity

For Richemont’s international CRC environment, this model supports scalable voice operations while maintaining cost transparency and commercial flexibility across global deployments.

Detailed country-specific pricing and applicable billing increments are provided within Twilio’s commercial proposal and associated pricing schedules.

## Q58: Can you provide one single consolidated global invoice in EUR for all the countries?

**Full Question**: Can you provide one single consolidated global invoice in EUR for all the countries?

**Importance**: High

**Answer**:

Global Invoicing & Billing Consolidation

Yes. Twilio can support centralized billing and invoicing models, including the ability to provide a single consolidated global invoice in EUR covering multiple countries and services, subject to final commercial structure and contracting arrangements.

Twilio recognises that multinational organisations such as Richemont often require simplified financial administration and centralized visibility across international communications environments.

Accordingly, Twilio can support billing models designed to provide:

A consolidated invoicing framework across participating countries and services
Centralized financial visibility and reporting
EUR-denominated invoicing where contractually agreed
Simplified billing administration and operational governance
Usage transparency across international deployments

Final invoicing design, country participation and applicable tax or regulatory considerations would be confirmed during commercial and contracting discussions to ensure alignment with Richemont’s preferred financial operating model and internal procurement requirements.
