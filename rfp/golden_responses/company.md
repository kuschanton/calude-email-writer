**Full Question**: In your response, present a detailed profile of your organization. Concurrently, elaborate on your company's vision for the market, including your long-term objectives, innovation roadmap, and how you foresee your role evolving within the industry.

**Importance**: High

**Answer**:

Company Profile, Market Vision & Innovation Strategy

Twilio Ireland Limited, part of Twilio Inc., delivers cloud communications and enterprise voice connectivity to organisations operating globally. Founded in 2008, Twilio has grown into a global, remote-first organisation with operational presence across North America, South America, Europe and Asia and more than 5,500 employees worldwide as of June, 2026.

At the core of Twilio’s communications platform sits the Twilio Super Network, a globally distributed communications network engineered to deliver secure, resilient and high-performance connectivity at enterprise scale. Combining extensive carrier relationships, distributed cloud infrastructure, intelligent routing and localized regulatory expertise, Twilio provides flexible and scalable communications infrastructure designed to support complex multinational environments.

For Richemont, this provides access to a globally available communications foundation supporting reliable SIP trunking, scalable number management and resilient voice operations across international CRC environments.

Twilio believes the communications market is undergoing a structural transformation driven by cloud infrastructure, AI, automation and rising customer expectations. Traditional telecom models built around fragmented infrastructure and static connectivity are increasingly giving way to programmable, software-defined communications platforms capable of supporting agility, operational resilience and personalised engagement at global scale.

Twilio’s long-term strategy is centred on enabling this transformation. Continued investment focuses on three core areas: strengthening global communications infrastructure and carrier connectivity, advancing intelligent routing and observability capabilities, and accelerating AI-enabled communications and programmable customer engagement.

Twilio sees its role evolving beyond traditional connectivity provision toward becoming a long-term technology partner supporting communications modernisation and future customer engagement strategies.

For Richemont, this means access not only to enterprise-grade communications infrastructure, but also to a collaborative technology partner committed to supporting operational resilience, scalable global communications and long-term innovation.

**Full Question**: Describe your competitive landscape, including your main competitors. Articulate your unique value proposition and detail the strategies and features that differentiate your company and its solutions

**Importance**: High

**Answer**:

Competitive Landscape & Twilio Differentiation

The enterprise communications market is highly competitive and continues to evolve rapidly as organisations modernise voice infrastructure and customer engagement capabilities.

Twilio operates within a landscape that includes traditional telecommunications providers, communications platform vendors and cloud contact centre and connectivity specialists. These organisations often differ in delivery models, infrastructure ownership, geographic coverage and platform flexibility.

While many providers offer connectivity and telephony services, Twilio differentiates through its programmable, cloud-native platform and globally distributed communications infrastructure.

At the core of Twilio’s differentiation sits the Twilio Super Network, a globally distributed communications network combining extensive carrier relationships, intelligent routing and localized regulatory expertise. This enables Twilio to provide resilient, scalable and globally available communications infrastructure designed to support complex multinational environments.

Twilio’s unique value proposition is built around several strategic differentiators:

Programmable & API-Driven Architecture – enabling flexible integration with enterprise platforms and avoiding traditional infrastructure rigidity
Global Infrastructure & Carrier Reach – supporting international operations through distributed connectivity and localized expertise
Intelligent Routing & Operational Resilience – helping optimise voice quality, latency and business continuity
Scalability & Deployment Flexibility – enabling organisations to adapt communications capacity and architecture as business needs evolve
Innovation & Future Readiness – supporting AI-enabled communications, intelligent routing and programmable engagement capabilities

Unlike traditional telecom approaches that may rely on fragmented infrastructure or fixed carrier relationships, Twilio provides a software-driven communications platform designed to evolve alongside customer requirements.
