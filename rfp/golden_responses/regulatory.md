## Q8: Describe your process for managing local regulatory requirements (KYC, Proof of Address) in restrict...

**Full Question**: Describe your process for managing local regulatory requirements (KYC, Proof of Address) in restrictive markets.

**Importance**: High

**Answer**:

Twilio recognises that telecommunications regulations, Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements and Proof of Address obligations vary significantly across jurisdictions and can represent a critical dependency within international number provisioning and portability programmes.

Twilio manages local regulatory requirements through a centralized Regulatory Compliance system (Trust Hub) that handles KYC, proof of address, and other documentation requirements across 180+ countries. This structured compliance and regulatory governance framework is designed to support predictable delivery while aligning with country-specific telecom regulations and operator policies. 

For restrictive markets, customers create Regulatory Bundles containing required documentation (business registration, proof of address, identity verification, authorized representative details). Twilio reviews submissions within 2-3 business days and provides clear rejection reasons if documentation is insufficient. The process supports both Console-based manual submission and REST API automation for scale.

When regulations change, Twilio proactively notifies affected customers via email and Console alerts, detailing what documents need updating and the compliance deadline. Customers update a copy of their bundle to avoid service disruption, and once approved, the updated information is applied to active phone numbers.

Country-specific requirements are published in Twilio's Regulatory Guidelines, covering documentation needs for each market, number type, and entity classification.

More information: https://help.twilio.com/articles/8338625205147-How-to-Submit-a-Regulatory-Bundle-for-Phone-Number-Regulatory-Compliance
Regulatory Guidelines: https://www.twilio.com/en-us/guidelines/regulatory

## Q10: What is your process for managing local regulatory updates ?

**Full Question**: What is your process for managing local regulatory updates ?

**Importance**: High

**Answer**:

Twilio proactively monitors global telecom regulations and notifies customers via email when local requirements change. Notifications specify which regulation changed, affected accounts, compliance deadlines, and include a direct link to the Console.

Customers update Regulatory Bundles through the Console by filtering bundles requiring updates, creating a copy to avoid service disruption, updating documents and information, and submitting for review. Twilio reviews submissions within 24 business hours, and once approved, updates are applied to active phone numbers.

This process ensures continuous compliance without interrupting phone number provisioning or active services.

More information: https://www.twilio.com/docs/phone-numbers/regulatory/getting-started/console-getting-started-compliance-information-update

## Q11: How do you inform your clients with local regulatory updates ? and how do you ensure that they becom...

**Full Question**: How do you inform your clients with local regulatory updates ? and how do you ensure that they become compliant with the new rules?

**Importance**: Medium

**Answer**:

Twilio informs clients through automated email notifications and Console alerts when regulatory requirements change. Notifications detail which regulation changed, affected accounts/bundles, compliance deadlines, and provide a direct link to take action.

To ensure compliance, Twilio guides customers through updating Regulatory Bundles via the Console: customers filter bundles requiring updates, create a copy to avoid service disruption, update required documents, and submit for review. Twilio reviews submissions within 24 business hours, and upon approval, applies updates to active phone numbers.

The Console displays compliance status in real-time, allowing customers to track bundle updates and resolve any issues promptly.

More information: https://www.twilio.com/docs/phone-numbers/regulatory/getting-started/console-getting-started-compliance-information-update
