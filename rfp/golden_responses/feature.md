## Q20: What is your product roadmap?

**Full Question**: What is your product roadmap?

**Importance**: Medium

**Answer**:

Twilio's product roadmap is dynamic and continuously evolving based on customer feedback, market demands, and innovation priorities. The roadmap is updated quarterly across all product areas including Voice, Messaging, Customer Data Platform (Segment), AI capabilities, and platform infrastructure.

Your dedicated account team will conduct Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs) to present roadmap updates, discuss upcoming features relevant to your use case, and gather feedback to influence future development priorities. This ensures you stay informed about new capabilities and can plan your integration roadmap accordingly.

For current product updates and recent launches, please visit Twilio's changelog and product announcements.

More information: https://www.twilio.com/en-us/changelog

## Q21: What are your current features around AI? What is your AI product roadmap?

**Full Question**: What are your current features around AI? What is your AI product roadmap?

**Importance**: Medium

**Answer**:

Twilio currently offers AI-powered products across voice and messaging:

Conversation Relay enables building AI voice agents with natural, human-like conversations. It handles speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and real-time voice interactions, integrating with your AI models and tech stack.

Conversation Intelligence (classic) provides AI-powered call transcription and language analysis, automatically transcribing voice and messaging conversations with sentiment analysis and actionable insights.

Real-time Transcription delivers live call transcription during active calls, enabling real-time analysis and agent assistance.

Looking ahead, Twilio's next-generation AI platform introduces three foundational capabilities:

Conversation Memory provides persistent customer context across all channels, automatically extracting observations, resolving customer identity, and surfacing context through semantic search - enabling both AI and human agents to access full customer history instantly.

Conversation Orchestrator connects every interaction into continuous conversations, coordinating routing and workflows as customers move between channels with seamless AI-to-human handoffs and cross-channel continuity.

Conversation Intelligence (next-gen) delivers real-time analysis of conversations, surfacing structured insights like summaries, sentiment, intent, and compliance signals for coaching and automation.

Twilio's AI vision: eliminate conversation amnesia by unifying communications, customer data, and AI on a model-agnostic platform that enables context-aware engagement at scale without vendor lock-in.
