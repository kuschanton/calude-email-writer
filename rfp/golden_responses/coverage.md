## Q6: Please detail your sourcing strategy for covering existing and new countries. Specifically, clarify ...

**Full Question**: Please detail your sourcing strategy for covering existing and new countries. Specifically, clarify whether your approach primarily involves direct relationships with carriers, the utilization of aggregators, or a hybrid model, and elaborate on the rationale behind your chosen strategy.

**Importance**: High

**Answer**:

Twilio uses a hybrid sourcing model combining direct carrier relationships with aggregator partnerships. In strategic markets (US, Canada, UK, Germany, France, Ireland, Italy, Australia, Japan, etc.), Twilio holds direct Tier-1 licenses and connects directly to local carriers for superior deliverability and regulatory compliance. In other markets, Twilio partners with trusted aggregators for rapid coverage and redundancy.

The Twilio Super Network dynamically routes each call or message through the best available path—whether direct or aggregated—based on real-time quality, cost, and compliance factors. With 4,800+ carrier connections and an average route depth of 4+ carriers per destination, this approach delivers global consistency, scalability, resilience, and local compliance.

For new countries, Twilio prioritizes direct relationships in high-value or regulated markets, while leveraging aggregators for speed-to-market elsewhere, continuously optimizing based on quality and customer demand.

More information: https://www.twilio.com/en-us/global-infrastructure
