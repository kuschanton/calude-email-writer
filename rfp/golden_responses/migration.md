## Q15: Describe how do you manage the migration of company as Richemont with 50+ countries and 1000+ teleph...

**Full Question**: Describe how do you manage the migration of company as Richemont with 50+ countries and 1000+ telephone numbers?

**Importance**: Critical

**Answer**:

Twilio manages large-scale, multi-country migrations like Richemont's through our premium Custom Porting service, which provides dedicated project management and white-glove support throughout the entire process.

Your dedicated project manager serves as a single point of contact, coordinating all aspects: conducting initial discovery to audit numbers by country and type, reviewing country-specific regulatory requirements and carrier relationships, creating a detailed migration plan with phased timelines and risk mitigation strategies, coordinating document collection (LOAs, invoices, regulatory forms) across all countries, managing communication with multiple losing carriers and regulatory authorities, executing bulk porting in batches prioritized by business needs, and conducting thorough post-port testing to ensure operational readiness.

Throughout the migration, you receive proactive communication with regular status updates, and can schedule meeting cadences to review progress. The project manager handles all escalations and works closely with your IT, telecom, and compliance teams to ensure minimal business disruption.

Custom Porting includes the ability to schedule port dates and times (where carrier restrictions allow), ensuring alignment with your operational requirements.

## Q16: Do you have a project team to manage the migration ? explain how you manage the project management?

**Full Question**: Do you have a project team to manage the migration ? explain how you manage the project management?

**Importance**: High

**Answer**:

Yes. Twilio's Custom Porting service provides a dedicated project team led by an experienced project manager who serves as your single point of contact throughout the migration.

The project manager coordinates all resources and activities: kickoff meetings to align on scope, timelines, and stakeholders, comprehensive project planning with country-by-country execution roadmap, regular status updates and scheduled meeting cadences to track progress, proactive escalation management leveraging Twilio's carrier relationships, document preparation and validation support (LOAs, invoices, regulatory forms), and post-migration testing and verification.

The team works closely with your IT, telecom, and compliance stakeholders to ensure seamless coordination and minimal disruption. You receive transparent communication at every phase, with clear visibility into porting status, issues, and next steps.

This hands-on, white-glove approach ensures complex multi-country migrations are executed smoothly, on schedule, and with clear accountability.
